About

Questions

Before your first visit

I've never tried reiki. Is that okay?

Yes. Many people arrive unsure whether it will work for them. You simply lie down and rest; Moon guides you through the session.

What happens in a somatic session?

It combines massage with natural essential oils, reiki, singing bowls and other tools in one session.

What if I get emotional?

That's normal and welcome. Some people cry during their first session and describe it as a release. There's always time to talk afterwards.

How do I book and pay?

Moon works by appointment only, so please don't walk in without booking. Send Moon a message on Instagram (or call) with the day and time you'd like. Sessions run from 10 AM to 12 PM, Tuesday to Friday. You pay in VND, by cash or QR code.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. Reiki and massage are complementary wellness practices for relaxation and care. They don't replace a doctor, and you should keep any treatment you're following.
